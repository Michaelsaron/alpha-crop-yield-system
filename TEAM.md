## Project
**CropYield — Ethiopian Smallholder Crop Yield Intelligence**

A reproducible machine-learning workflow that cleans farm data, integrates growing-season weather, analyzes agronomic and market patterns, predicts crop yield, and converts predictions into approximate revenue insight without using market price as a model feature.
